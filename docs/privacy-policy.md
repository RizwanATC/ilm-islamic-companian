# Ilm privacy policy

_Last updated: 2 November 2026_

Ilm is a prayer times, Quran and daily reminder app. It has no sign-in, no accounts, no ads
and no analytics. The name you give the app is saved on your phone only and never sent anywhere. This page explains what leaves your phone and why.

## Location

Ilm uses your location to work out your prayer times. Your exact position stays on your phone:

- **In Malaysia**, the app finds your JAKIM prayer zone on the phone and sends only the zone
  code (for example `WLY01`) to JAKIM e-Solat (e-solat.gov.my) to download that month's
  official times.
- **Outside Malaysia**, times are calculated on the phone and nothing is sent.

You can turn location off and pick a zone by hand in Settings.

## Quran text and recitation

When you open a surah for the first time, the app downloads its text from api.alquran.cloud
and saves it on your phone. Recitation audio streams from everyayah.com. These requests
contain only which surah or ayah you asked for.

## Chat with Yun or Ayu

When you send a message in the chat, the conversation so far, the
place name shown in the app, and today's prayer times are sent to our server and passed to
Anthropic's Claude to write the reply. Messages are not used to identify you and are not
stored by Ilm after the reply is sent. Anthropic processes them under its commercial terms,
which do not allow training on this data. Don't share sensitive personal details in the chat.

## Data saved on your phone

Your settings, prayer marks, bookmarks, reading position and downloaded surahs are stored
only on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

## Notifications

Prayer alerts are scheduled on your phone. No server sends them.

## Children

Ilm is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
